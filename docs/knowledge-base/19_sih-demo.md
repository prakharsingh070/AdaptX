# SIH Demonstration

## Demonstration Story

1. Start in a normal environment.
2. Show the coarse map.
3. Introduce a vehicle.
4. Show tracking beginning.
5. Detect a pedestrian.
6. Show risk increasing.
7. Update the predicted trajectory.
8. Predict a future conflict.
9. Expand the risk heatmap.
10. Increase adaptive resolution in the critical region.
11. Show the high-detail region.
12. Let the threat disappear or move away.
13. Show resolution decreasing.
14. Compare fixed-resolution and ADAPT-X performance.
15. Show measured computational and perception results.

## Demonstration Rules

The dashboard should show the reason behind resolution changes. Simulation and replay states must be labelled. The final story must be backed by actual system events and measurements rather than hardcoded claims.
